As AI systems become general-purpose interpreters of human intent, everyone who works with them is writing specifications. The gap between what a person *means* and what they *specify* is where failures occur, and it is invisible from the inside. This draft framework argues that specification literacy belongs alongside reading, writing, and numeracy, traces how it develops from kindergarten through working life, and pilots it first with an adult cohort at De Anza College.
